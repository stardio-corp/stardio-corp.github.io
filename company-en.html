01 / ABOUT STARDIO

Creating media through curiosity.

Stardio is a creative brand built around observing the things we love in depth and sharing what makes them interesting from our own perspective.

The name “Stardio” combines “Star” and “Studio.” We turn our interests and discoveries into content to share with others.

Stardio currently runs two stationery projects: “People Stationery” and “Studio People.” Each shares the appeal and knowledge of stationery in its own way.
